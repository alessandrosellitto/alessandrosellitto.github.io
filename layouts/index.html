Alessandro Sellitto

Postdoctoral Researcher, Institut Mines-Télécom Business School
PhD Candidate, University of Milan & University of Pavia

I am a Postdoctoral Researcher in Computational Economics at Institut Mines-Télécom Business School and a PhD candidate at the University of Milan and the University of Pavia. I am also a Research Affiliate at the Düsseldorf Institute for Competition Economics (DICE).

My research lies at the intersection of industrial organization, digital economics, and applied microeconomic theory. I study how data, algorithms, and AI shape competition, platform incentives, and consumer welfare in digital markets.

You can contact me at: alessandro.sellitto@imt-bs.eu or alessandro.sellitto@unimi.it.
